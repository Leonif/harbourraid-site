In short

Harbour Raid does not collect, store on servers or share any data about you. The game has no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party libraries, and it makes no network requests.

What is kept on your Mac

  • Best scores and unlocked levels.
  • Settings: language, sound, music.
  • Technical sortie statistics (hits, score, duration) - a file in the game's data used only for balance tuning; it is never sent anywhere.

This data stays in the game's container on your device. To remove it, delete the game together with its data.

Purchase

Buying and downloading the game is handled by Apple through the Mac App Store under Apple's own privacy policy. The developer receives none of your payment or personal data.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, children included.
