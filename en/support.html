Controls

MouseMoves the plane; the cursor is hidden and captured in flight
Left buttonFire (hold)
Right buttonBomb
Arrows / WASDMove
Space / ZFire (hold)
X / BBomb
P / EscPause; the mouse is released
1 / 2Switch effects / music off or on

Common questions

How do I change the language? Menu - Settings - Language. Ukrainian and English are available.

The cursor is gone and will not move. The game captures the mouse in flight. Press P or Esc: the pause gives the cursor back.

Where are my best scores kept? Only on your Mac, in the game's data. Deleting the game with its data removes them.

What does it run on? macOS 13 or later, a Mac with Apple silicon or Intel.
